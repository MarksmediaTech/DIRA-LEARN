## Digital Marketing

**Final project:** A basic digital marketing campaign for a real brief (a local business, youth enterprise, community organisation or the learner's own idea). It contains a SMART objective, a customer persona, a channel plan, a brand mini-kit, a two-week content calendar, at least three finished posts with captions, a WhatsApp Business plan, a small KES budget, the measures of success and a short results or test report, presented in session 8. Privacy option: a learner who cannot or does not wish to publish publicly may run the campaign as a private test (peers, or a small WhatsApp group whose members agreed to take part) or build it for the simulated brief provided by the facilitator, with mock-up posts and clearly labelled estimated results; it is assessed on the same rubric. A real business's name, logo, photos, prices or customer information are used only with the owner's written permission.

## AI Tools

**Final project:** An AI-assisted solution or demonstration for a real brief (a study resource pack, a customer-service prompt kit for a small business, an AI-assisted marketing or information pack for a community group, or a simple automated workflow). It includes the problem and user, the AI tools and prompts used, the human checks, an evaluation against the AI quality checklist, a user test and a live demonstration in session 8 with an AI-use disclosure. Privacy option: the solution can be tested with a classmate or with invented sample data instead of a real user. No personal or business information goes into an AI tool without permission, and no AI image shows an identifiable real person without their consent.

## Content Creation

Portfolio submissions for the programme use a restricted-access link shared only with named reviewers (for example a Google Drive folder set to "Restricted" and shared with the facilitator's email) or files uploaded in DIRA LEARN - never a public or "anyone with the link" link. A public portfolio is the learner's own optional choice, after consent checks. Privacy option: the brief can be simulated. Identifiable people appear only with consent recorded in the credit and consent log.

It includes an opportunity statement backed by customer interviews, a value proposition, a one-page business model, a start-up budget and one-month cash-flow estimate with pricing, a one-page marketing and customer-experience plan, evidence from at least one real test, a 3-minute pitch in session 8 and a 30-day validation action. Privacy option: the business can be a simulated idea, and customer evidence is shown in the pitch without names, photos or contact details unless the person agreed.
