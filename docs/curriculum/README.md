# DIRA LEARN V13.2 curriculum – overview

Revised 19 Nov 2026. Four weeks, eight live sessions, two a week, at most two hours each. Week 1 is the shared core; weeks 2 to 4 are specialist-track sessions (sessions 3 to 8).

## Specialist tracks

### Digital Marketing
Reach and win customers online. Learners build a business's online presence, understand their audience, plan and design content, write persuasive copy, use WhatsApp Business, search and simple advertising, measure results, and finish by building and presenting a basic digital marketing campaign for a real Kenyan business, cause or their own idea.

**Final project:** A basic digital marketing campaign for a real brief (a local business, youth enterprise, community organisation or the learner's own idea). It contains a SMART objective, a customer persona, a channel plan, a brand mini-kit, a two-week content calendar, at least three finished posts with captions, a WhatsApp Business plan, a small KES budget, the measures of success and a short results or test report, presented in session 8. Privacy option: a learner who cannot or does not wish to publish publicly may run the campaign as a private test (peers, or a small WhatsApp group whose members agreed to take part) or build it for the simulated brief provided by the facilitator, with mock-up posts and clearly labelled estimated results; it is assessed on the same rubric. A real business's name, logo, photos, prices or customer information are used only with the owner's written permission.

### AI Tools
Work smarter with AI, responsibly. Learners understand how AI tools work, write strong prompts, use AI for research, writing, images, presentations, productivity and business tasks, learn simple automation, check AI outputs for accuracy, privacy and bias, and finish by building and demonstrating an AI-assisted solution for a real need.

**Final project:** An AI-assisted solution or demonstration for a real brief (a study resource pack, a customer-service prompt kit for a small business, an AI-assisted marketing or information pack for a community group, or a simple automated workflow). It includes the problem and user, the AI tools and prompts used, the human checks, an evaluation against the AI quality checklist, a user test and a live demonstration in session 8 with an AI-use disclosure. Privacy option: the solution can be tested with a classmate or with invented sample data instead of a real user. No personal or business information goes into an AI tool without permission, and no AI image shows an identifiable real person without their consent.

### Content Creation
Tell stories people stop for. Learners plan content with a strategy, tell stories, shoot photos and video on a phone with good light, composition and sound, edit video and audio, write captions, design graphics with a consistent brand, use content responsibly and legally, and finish with a content portfolio and a creative project for a real brief.

**Final project:** A content portfolio built around one creative project for a real brief (a business, a cause, a community or the learner's own creator brand). It contains a content strategy one-pager, at least one edited short video with captions, a photo set, a branded graphic or carousel, a credit and consent log, and a portfolio page or folder with short case-study notes, presented in session 8 with a 30-day content plan. Portfolio submissions for the programme use a restricted-access link shared only with named reviewers (for example a Google Drive folder set to "Restricted" and shared with the facilitator's email) or files uploaded in DIRA LEARN - never a public or "anyone with the link" link. A public portfolio is the learner's own optional choice, after consent checks. Privacy option: the brief can be simulated. Identifiable people appear only with consent recorded in the credit and consent log.

### Entrepreneurship & Business
Turn an idea into a tested business concept. Learners spot opportunities, understand customer problems and markets, design a value proposition and business model, learn practical money skills (costing, pricing, revenue, expenses and cash flow), market and sell ethically with good customer service, test their idea with real customers, and pitch a business concept.

**Final project:** A business concept and pitch for an early-stage idea. It includes an opportunity statement backed by customer interviews, a value proposition, a one-page business model, a start-up budget and one-month cash-flow estimate with pricing, a one-page marketing and customer-experience plan, evidence from at least one real test, a 3-minute pitch in session 8 and a 30-day validation action. Privacy option: the business can be a simulated idea, and customer evidence is shown in the pitch without names, photos or contact details unless the person agreed.
